(palette, sky, sun, fog, lakes, Biome variants, Biome Transitions, terrain detail) in spec 001 are
complete, unambiguous, consistent, measurable, and traceable enough to drive `/speckit-tasks`.

**Feature**: [spec.md](../spec.md) (User Story 2, FR-016 to FR-025, SC-003/005/006, Edge Cases,
Assumptions); [plan.md](../plan.md) and [research.md](../research.md) R1-R6 for consistency checks.

**Marker Semantics**: `[x]` means the criterion has been reviewed and satisfied for requirements quality. It does not mean implementation work is complete.
